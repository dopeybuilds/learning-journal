Hi, my name is Victor and welcome to my learning journal

After weeks of contemplation I have decided to start my journey as a developer starting with basic HTML, CSS, finally getting into JavaScript. I would like to connect with like minded peers throughout this process and get reviews of my code by mentors in the field.

How I stay commited to learning

What really helps me stay commited to learning is slowly watching my process evolve baby steps. Adding onto older projects, learning new concepts, watching my projects evolve as I learn new skills. I have learned that this is a craft and I have to start from the bottom. The climb is the motivation.

How I got started

It started slow, I have always been interested in the programming/web development world. Over the years I have looked at code and it all just seemed overwhelming. After much contemplation I decided to take some courses on Scrimba, the platform is very interactive, does not throw you off the deep end but slowly gets you familiar with manipulating code and creating things. It is the perfect platform for learners who are more hands on with things.
